Draft. This policy hasn’t had a lawyer’s read yet. It describes what the app really does today.

The short version

  • We collect what Gossoko needs to work: your email, a username, and what you post (reviews, ratings, photos, saved spots, deal codes).
  • Your phone’s location is used on your phone to work out how far spots are. We don’t send it to our servers and we don’t track where you go.
  • No ads, no ad trackers, and we don’t sell or rent your information.
  • You can delete your account in the app (Profile, then Delete my account) and it removes your account and what you posted.

What we collect

When you make an account

  • Email address and password. Your password is handled by our sign-in provider (Supabase Auth) and is stored only as a one-way hash. We can’t read it.
  • Username. Optional: full name, bio, trade and a profile photo.

What you do in the app

  • Reviews, ratings, comments, replies and photos you post. These are public and show your username.
  • Likes, saved spots (private to you) and reports you make about content.
  • Deal codes you get (the code, the deal, the venue and when it was issued and used).
  • Alerts we send you inside the app, and your alert settings.
  • Venues you add, including the address and the map position of the venue.

If you run a venue

  • Venue claims and the details we use to check you own the place, your listing, deals, replies and any Pro request or plan status.
  • Your dashboard shows counts (views, saves, directions, calls, shares, codes), not the names of the people behind them.
  • Pro payments are taken by Stripe (see below). We see that a subscription is active, not your card number.

On your phone or browser

  • Sign-in session. If you choose Remember me in the iPhone, iPad or Android app, your session is kept in the phone’s secure storage (Keychain or Keystore). On the web it’s kept in your browser’s storage. Signing out clears it.
  • Push token. If you allow notifications in the app, your phone gives us a token so we can send you alerts. We tie it to your account and release it when you sign out.
  • A random device ID, kept in your browser or app storage. It lets us count one view or tap per venue per day without knowing who you are. It isn’t linked to your name or email, and the daily tally behind it is cleared within a few days.

What we don’t do

  • No advertising, no ad networks, no analytics or tracking software from other companies in the app.
  • We don’t sell your information or use it to build ad profiles.
  • We don’t ask for your contacts, microphone or camera. If you add a photo you pick it yourself.

Your location

If you allow it, Gossoko asks your phone or browser for your location while you’re using the app, so it can show how far each venue is and sort Nearby. The distance is worked out on your device. Your position is not sent to our servers or stored. Say no and the app still works, just without distances. You can turn it off any time in your phone or browser settings.

The one exception is when you add a new venue and tap “Use my location” to fill in the venue’s map position. That position is saved as the venue’s location and is public. It’s where the venue is, not a record of you.

The map is drawn using map tiles from OpenFreeMap. When you open the map, your device asks their servers for the tiles for the area on screen, so they can see your device’s IP address and roughly which area you’re looking at.

Check-ins and arrival alerts (not in the app yet)

We’re planning an optional check-in feature. If you turn on check-ins in Settings, the app will explain what it does and then ask for location permission. With it you could check in at a venue, get an alert when you arrive near one, and be asked for a review afterwards. When it launches:

  • It will be off until you switch it on, and you can switch it off any time.
  • Your live position will stay on your phone. Only a check-in (which venue and when) would be sent to us, and we’ll check it against the venue’s position so people can’t check in from home.
  • You’ll be able to delete your check-ins, and they’re deleted with your account.
  • Arrival alerts may need “Always” location permission on your phone. We’ll only ask if you turn that part on.

Why we use it

  • To run Gossoko: sign you in, show reviews and rankings, keep your saved spots and deal codes, and send alerts you’ve asked for.
  • To keep it fair and safe: spot spam and abuse, hold or hide posts, handle reports, and check venue claims.
  • To give venue owners their dashboard, and to bill for paid features.
  • To meet the law and to answer your requests.

If we ever want to use your information for something different, we’ll ask first or tell you.

Who sees your information

  • Everyone: your username, reviews, ratings, comments, photos (once approved), and anything you put on a venue listing.
  • Venue owners: reviews and reports about their venue, and a deal code if you show it at the counter. They don’t get your email.
  • Our moderators and admins: accounts, reports, held posts and claims, so they can do their job. Moderation actions are logged.
  • Nobody else unless the law requires it (for example a court order) or you’ve agreed.

The companies that help us run Gossoko

We use these providers. They handle information for us under their own privacy terms.

  • Supabase: our database, sign-in, file storage and server functions. Our project is in the Sydney, Australia region (ap-southeast-2).
  • Stripe: card payments for venue Pro, once switched on. Stripe collects the card details and the billing email. We don’t see or store card numbers.
  • Apple (APNs) and Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging): they carry push notifications to iPhones, iPads and Android phones. They get your push token and the notification text.
  • HostGator: hosts our website files at sinsera.co.
  • Our email server (mail.sinsera.co): sends the weekly summary email to venue owners, once that is switched on.
  • OpenFreeMap: map tiles (see Your location).
  • Apple Maps or Google Maps: if you tap Directions, your phone opens that app or site with the venue’s address. That is their service and their terms.

Some of these (Stripe, Apple, Google, our hosting provider and OpenFreeMap) may handle information outside Australia, including in the United States and Europe. We only use providers we think take reasonable care of it. Supabase stores your Gossoko data in Australia.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and what you posted stay until you delete them or your account.
  • Deal codes expire after 2 hours, though a record of the code stays on the venue’s usage sheet while your account exists.
  • The daily device tally is cleared within a few days.
  • Your push token is released when you sign out, and removed when you delete your account.
  • Moderation and audit records (who did what, and why) are kept as long as we need them to run the service fairly. When your account is deleted they are kept without your name linked to them.
  • Billing records may be kept as long as tax law requires.
  • Our database backups roll over on a schedule, so deleted data leaves them over time.

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile, then Delete my account, type DELETE to confirm. It can’t be undone. This removes:

  • your login (email and password hash) and your profile;
  • your reviews, comments, likes and saved spots;
  • your deal codes, venue claims and Pro requests;
  • your alerts and your push tokens;
  • the photos you uploaded: the image files in our storage and their records.

What stays: venues you added stay on Gossoko, no longer linked to you. Reports you made, and moderation records, stay without your name. Admin and moderator accounts need their role changed by an owner first. If you can’t use the app, email us from the address on your account and we’ll do it for you.

Reports and moderation

Anyone can report a review, comment, photo or venue. A moderator looks at each report. Posts with links, phone numbers, emails, spam words or accusations are held for a check before they show, and sometimes all new posts are (we can switch that on). Posts that get enough reports from established accounts are hidden until checked. Moderators can suspend accounts that break the rules.

Keeping it safe

Data travels encrypted. Our database has row-level security, so you can only change your own things, and the powerful keys live on our servers, not in the app. Nothing is perfect, so please use a strong, unique password.

If there’s a data breach likely to cause serious harm, we’ll tell the people affected and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC), as the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme requires.

Kids

Gossoko isn’t directed at anyone under 16, and you need to be 16 or over to make an account. If you think a child under 16 has an account, email us and we’ll remove it.
