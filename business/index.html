The counter QR

Stick it up. Let it work.

  1. Print it from your dashboard (Counter QR, then Print, from a computer).
  2. Stick it on the counter, the till or the van window.
  3. Customers scan it and land on your page: they rate you, save you and grab your deal code.
  4. You check the code at the counter: type it into your dashboard and it’s used up.

More reviews push you up the rankings, and the rankings bring the next crew in.

Questions

Fair questions.

Is it really free to be listed?

Yes. Anyone can add a venue and anyone can review it. Claiming it, replying, editing your details, one deal at a time and the counter QR are all free.

Do tradies pay anything?

No. Reading, reviewing and grabbing deals are free for them, always. That’s what keeps the reviews coming.

Can I take down a bad review?

No, and nobody else can pay to either: that’s what makes the reviews worth reading. You can reply under any review, and report one that breaks the rules (abuse, spam, accusations). A moderator checks every report.

How do I prove I own the place?

Tap Claim on your venue’s page. We check before approving, and you get the verified owner badge and your dashboard.

How do I pay for Pro, and can I stop?

From your dashboard on the website, monthly. You can cancel any time. While card payments are being switched on, tap Ask for Pro in your dashboard and we’ll sort it out with you.

What does Featured get me, and how do I pay?

Featured puts your venue in a labelled “Promoted” spot at the top of Nearby and Search, for a week or a month. It doesn’t touch your rating or your place in Rankings, and there are only two spots at a time. We arrange it with you and send an invoice. Once it’s paid we switch it on. No card checkout for Featured yet, and no commission, ever.

Do I need the app?

No. Claiming, the dashboard, deals and the QR all work in a web browser. The phone apps are coming to the App Store and Google Play.

Where does Gossoko run?

South-East Queensland, starting around Brisbane. Most places on Gossoko today are in Brisbane, Logan and Moreton Bay, with more being added. Run a place somewhere else in South-East Queensland, like Ipswich or Redlands? Add it and it’ll show for tradies nearby.
