Gossoko for Android

Find the best tradie feed near the site. This is the early Android app, straight from us while the Play Store listing is on its way.

Download the app

About 8 MB. Android 7 or newer.

  1. Tap Download the app on your Android phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps.
  3. Tap Install, then open Gossoko and create your account.

On an iPhone or a computer? Use Gossoko in your browser.
