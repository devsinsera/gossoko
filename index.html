How it works

Find it. Grab a deal. Rate it.

  1. Find a spot

    The Nearby map shows what’s close and what’s open right now. Filter by type, sort by the score you care about, and tap Directions.

  2. Grab a deal code

    Venues post deals like “pie and coffee before 7”. Tap Get code, show the GS-code at the counter, done.

  3. Rate it

    Seven quick scores and a line or two. Your reviews help the next crew, and the place can reply.
